# LEARN

LEARN is a crowdsourced knowledge platform that connects academic concepts to their real-world applications across multiple domains.

Unlike traditional learning platforms that focus primarily on theory, LEARN is designed to answer a more practical question:

Where is this concept actually used in real life?

The platform allows users to explore concepts from fields such as science, engineering, medicine, economics, and more, and discover how they are applied in real-world scenarios. At the same time, it enables knowledgeable users, including students, educators, and professionals, to contribute structured applications with supporting media such as images and videos.

## What Makes LEARN Different

LEARN is not just another educational platform or content repository. Its uniqueness lies in the following:

* Application-First Learning
  Instead of focusing only on definitions and theory, LEARN emphasizes real-world usage, helping users understand the practical relevance of concepts.

* Community-Driven Knowledge
  Users can contribute applications, making the platform continuously evolve with real insights from different fields and industries.

* Structured Contributions
  Every application is added in a structured format (concept, explanation, domain, media), ensuring clarity and consistency unlike unstructured platforms.

* Wide Multi-Domain Coverage
  The platform spans across disciplines including science, engineering, healthcare, business, and humanities, enabling cross-domain learning.

* Built-in Moderation and Credibility
  Features like reporting, verification, and voting ensure content quality and reliability.
  
 # Difficulty-Based Learning
 Applications are categorized into Fundamental, Intermediate, and Advanced levels, allowing users to explore concepts based on their understanding and learning stage.

## Problem It Solves

Students often learn concepts without understanding their real-world relevance. This leads to:

* Lack of engagement
* Poor retention
* Difficulty applying knowledge

LEARN bridges this gap by directly linking concepts to their practical applications, making learning more intuitive, engaging, and meaningful.
